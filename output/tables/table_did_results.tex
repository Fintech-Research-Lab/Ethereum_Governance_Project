 \caption{The Effect of EIP Authorship on Seniority and Compensation}
 \label{tab:did_main}
     \begin{small}
    \begin{minipage}{\textwidth}
      The table reports covariate balance and difference-in-differences estimates of the effect of authoring an Ethereum Improvement Proposal (EIP) on career outcomes over a $\pm2$-year event window. An author is treated if they propose an EIP at t=0. Each treated author is matched to at most three similar control authors drawn from the population of EIP authors who author no EIP anywhere in the $\pm2$-year window; Panel A shows the results of a balance test. Panel B and C report stack diff-in-diff results for Seniority and Total Compensation (log) as outcome variable. Column~(1) reports the static model. Column~(2) replaces it with separate coefficients for each event year, $t-1$ omitted as the reference. Columns~(3) and~(4) re-run column~(1) on the two halves of the sample, split at the median of seniority in the year before authorship. Standard errors, reported in parentheses, are clustered at the author level; $^{*}$, $^{**}$, and $^{***}$ denote statistical significance at the 10\%, 5\%, and 1\% levels, respectively.
