\opening{Dear Will,}

Thank you for the opportunity to revise our paper, ``Decentralized Crypto Governance? Transparency and Concentration in Ethereum Decision-Making,'' co-authored with Moazzam Khoja and Fabian Sch\"ar, and submit it for publication in the regular-issue of Management Science.

We are grateful for the guidance of you, the associate editor, and the reviewers. Following your suggestions, we have substantially revised the paper in three ways:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
  \item New economic framework. We replaced the framework underlying our empirical analysis with one grounded in the corporate governance literature.
  \item New labor market evidence. We added a study of how contributing to Ethereum governance affects contributors' labor market outcomes.
  \item Streamlined analysis. We kept only the tests that map directly into the new framework and removed the rest.
\end{enumerate}

A detailed point-by-point response to each comment accompanies this letter. We believe the paper is much stronger as a result and makes a valuable contribution to the literature on cryptoasset governance and regulation.

Thank you for your time and consideration.


\closing{Sincerely,}
