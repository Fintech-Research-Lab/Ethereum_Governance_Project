\opening{Dear Tony,}

I am pleased to submit our paper, ``Decentralized Crypto Governance? Transparency and Concentration in Ethereum Decision-Making,'' co-authored with Moazzam Khoja and Fabian Schar, for consideration for publication in the Journal of Financial Economics.

This paper presents the first comprehensive analysis of the governance of Ethereum, the leading programmable blockchain for financial protocols and assets. We examine the decision-making process around protocol upgrades, identifying areas of both notable transparency and concerning centralization. Specifically, we find that the introduction of protocol changes is shaped by a core group of influential authors and entities, including the Ethereum Foundation. This concentration of influence contrasts with the high degree of transparency and community engagement observed during the discussion and finalization of upgrades.

These findings have significant implications for how cryptoassets should be regulated, particularly whether the presence of agency problems and potential insider information should need additional disclosure requirements or not.

While we have no strong preference for a specific editor, we believe Philipp Schnabl's expertise in financial intermediation and technology may make him particularly well-suited to evaluate our manuscript.

We believe this work makes a valuable contribution to the nascent literature on cryptoasset regulation and would be honored to have it published in the Journal of Financial Economics.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

\closing{Sincerely,}
